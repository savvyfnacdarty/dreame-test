Repair notice

Important Safety Instructions:
1. Wear gloves when carrying out any repair work.
2. Ensure that there are no exposed electrical wires in the maintenance environment and strictly prohibit direct testing of the equipment with AC power to prevent electric shock.
3. When repairing electrical components, please disconnect the battery pack first.

Preparation:
Empty the clean water tank and dirty water tank before disassembly.

Replacement requirements:
1. All parts that have passed the cross-check must be removed from the faulty machine, fitted with the user's original adapter and then subjected to the next replacement test.
2. After repair, organize the removed old parts and unused parts.

Tear down Guide

  1. Tear down handle

    1. Pull the handle up after holding according to photos as below

  1. Tear down Display Screen（Deco Cover、Display Screen）

    1. Pry up and disassemble Body Tube Deco Cover

  1. Remove screw and disassemble Body TOP DECO Cover

  1. Pry up and Clockwise rotation to teardown display screen

  1. Tear down battery

    1. Remove Screws and disassemble white plastic part

  1. Unplug the terminals and disassemble the battery

  1. Tear down Main Motor

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~3.0

    2. Remove Screws to disassemble the main body

  1. Remove the screws and disassemble the Main Motor

  1. Teardown Assy Brush Part（scraper、brush roller motor、water value、pump、Water level sensor、charging wire part）

    1. Unscrew 4pcs screws and disassembly scraper

  1. Unscrew Screws and disassembly cover

  1. Remove the deco patch and screws to disassemble the brush roller motor

  1. Remove screws and disassemble the plastic part

  1. Unplug the pipe to disassemble the water value

  1. Unplug the terminal to disassemble the pump

  1. Unplug the pipe to disassemble the Water level sensor

  1. Remove screws to disassemble the Brush charging wire part

  1. Remove screws to disassemble the brush bottom

  1. Teardown Assy Body Part（Rotary union、Main PCBA、Dirt sensor）

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~5.0

    2. Disassembly Rotary union

  1. Remove screws to disassemble the body front cover

  1. Remove screws and unplug terminals to disassemble the Main PCBA

  1. Remove screws and unplug terminal to disassemble the Dirt sensor
