Tear down Guide

  1. Tear down Accessory

    1. Disassembly all accessories

  1. Tear down Assy brush part

    1. Unscrew screws and take out scraper

  1. Unscrew screws and take out brush cover

  1. Unscrew screws and disassembly brush roller motor（including belt）

  1. Unscrew screws and disassembly pump

  1. Pull the wheel to disassembly it

  1. Tear down housing body

    1. Refer to the screen tearing down steps (2.0） to disassembly Assy brush part

    2. Unscrew 10pcs screws.

  1. Disassembly housing body

  1. Tear down Main Motor/Battery/PCBA

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~3.0 to disassembly the Assy brush part & housing body

    2. Spread the housing body and unplug terminal to take out Assy Main Motor

  2. Unplug the terminal and remove battery

  1. Unplug the terminal and remove PCBA

  1. Tear down handle

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~4.0 to disassembly the Assy brush part & housing body&PCBA

    2. Remove screws and disassembly handle
