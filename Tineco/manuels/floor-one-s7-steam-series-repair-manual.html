Repair notice

Important Safety Instructions:
1. Wear gloves when carrying out any repair work.
2. Ensure that there are no exposed electrical wires in the maintenance environment and strictly prohibit direct testing of the equipment with AC power to prevent electric shock.
3. When repairing electrical components, please disconnect the battery pack first.

Preparation:
Empty the clean water tank and dirty water tank before disassembly.

Replacement requirements:
1. All parts that have passed the cross-check must be removed from the faulty machine, fitted with the user's original adapter and then subjected to the next replacement test.
2. After repair, organise the removed old parts and unused parts.

Chapter 1:Main body teardown guide

Main body overview

Front elevation Back elevation

Screen disassembly and assembly

1.1 Disassembly guide

1.1.1 Rotate the screen counterclockwise from left to right, until the screen latches be released in place as shown in the photo.

1.1.2 Press the terminal latch to disassembly the screen

1.2 Assembly guide

1.2.1 Assembly the terminal to connect the screen wire

1.2.2 Rotate the scree to locked. Make sure there is no misalignment at the position marked on the photo.

Up-front Cover Disassembly and Assembly

2.1 Disassembly

2.1.1 Refer to the screen tearing down steps (1.1） to disassembly the screen, and unscrew the two screws.

2.1.2 Unscrew the screw of up-front cover 2.1.3 Use tool to pry open the front cover

2.2 Assembly

2.2.1 Connect the terminal of DWT sensor and insert the wires into the wire groove. Snap the up-front cover onto the main body.

2.2.2 Screw two screws 2.2.3 Screw the two screws

2.2.2 Refer to the screen installation steps (1.2) to install the screen, being careful not to pinch the wires.

PCBA Disassembly And Assembly

3.1 Disassembly

3.1.1 Refer to the screen tearing down steps (1.1） to disassembly the screen.

3.1.2 Remove the support board, cut off the zip tie. 3.1.3 Unscrew the screw, and

Remove all the terminals. remove the PCBA

3.2 Assembly

3.2.1 Screw in two screws of the PCBA 3.2.2 Insert all the terminals, and arrange and tie them with zip tie.

3.2.3 Install the support board.

3.2.4 Refer to the screen installation steps (1.2) to install the screen, being careful not to pinch the wires

Motor & Battery Disassembly and Assembly

4.1 Disassembly

4.1.1 Refer to step 2.1 to remove the up-front cover and screen, and unscrew 2 screws marked on the photo.

4.1.2 Unscrew the screws and tear down 4.1.3 Cut the zip ties, then disconnect the the battery pack motor assembly. positive, negative, and signal wire terminals.

4.1.4 Disconnect the terminals of the motor and battery pack，and disassemble the battery pack and motor assembly.

4.1.5 Use a tool to unlock the clasp.and then the motor and battery will be seperated.

Bartery Motor

4.2 Assembly

4.2.1 Thread the motor's wire harness through the center of the battery pack 

4.2.2 Align the arrows on the battery pack with the motor housing as marked on the photo, then lock the 3 clasps to assemble the motor and battery pack together.

4.2.2 Connect the battery terminals. 4.2.3 Connect the motor terminals

and tie the wires with zip

4.2.4 Tighten 2 screws of PCBA 4.2.5 Install the assembly into the main body.

Tighten 2 screws.

4.2.6 Refer to step 2.2 to install the up-front cover and screen.

Iloop sensor disassembly and assembly

5.1 Disassembly

5.1.1 Refer to 4.1(4.1.1~4.1.2) to remove the battery and motor assembly. and cut the wires’ zip ties.

5.1.2 Unscrew the two screws 5.1.3 Unscrew the four screws of rotating bracket

5.1.4 Unscrew two screws 5.1.5 remove the down-front cover

5.1.6 Remove two screws and disconnect the sensor terminals on the PCBA, then remove the sensor.

5.2 Assembly

5.2.1 Install the sensor making sure the scrap in position and tighten 2 screws

5.2.2 Insert the wire into the slot and install the front cover, then screw it in place.

5.2.3 Screw the two screws 5.2.4 Install the rotating bracket in place and screw it

5.2.5 Connect the terminal of sensor

5.2.6 Refer to 4.2.5 to install the battery and motor assembly and2.2 to install up-front cover

5.2.6 Tie up the wire zip and install the screen refer to step 1.2.

Clean Water Sensor& Pump disassembly and assembly

6.1 Disassembly

6.1.1 Refer to 5.1.1~5.1.5 to remove the down-front cover and the rotating bracket

6.1.2 Remove the charging contact conductive component

6.1.3 Unscrew two screws 6.1.4 Unscrew the screw

6.1.5 Remove the two water pipe connectors，and disconnect the terminal of the pump and the sensor.

Sensor Pump

6.2 Assembly

6.2.1 Install the sensor, making sure the scrap in position, and screw it with three screws.

6.2.2 Install the pump, and screw it in. and connect two water pipe terminals.

6.2.3 Install the charging contact conductive component.

6.2.4 Arrange the wiring harness and water pipes.

6.2.5 Refer to step 6.1.5 to connect the terminals of the sensor and the pump.

6.2.6 Refer to step 5.2.2~5.2.6 to install the Iloop sensor.

6.2.7 Tie up the wire zip and install the screen refer to step 1.2
