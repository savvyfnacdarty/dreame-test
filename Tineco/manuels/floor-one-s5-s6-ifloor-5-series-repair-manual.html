Repair notice

Important Safety Instructions:
1. Wear gloves when carrying out any repair work.
2. Ensure that there are no exposed electrical wires in the maintenance environment and strictly prohibit direct testing of the equipment with AC power to prevent electric shock.
3. When repairing electrical components, please disconnect the battery pack first.

Preparation:
Empty the clean water tank and dirty water tank before disassembly.

Replacement requirements:
1. All parts that have passed the cross-check must be removed from the faulty machine, fitted with the user's original adapter and then subjected to the next replacement test.
2. After repair, organise the removed old parts and unused parts.

Tear down Guide

  1. Tear down handle& Accessory

    1. Pull the handle up after holding according to photos as below

  1. Disassembly all accessories

  1. Tear down Display Screen

    1. Clockwise rotation to teardown display screen

    2. Unplug terminal pins of display screen

  1. Tear down Main PCBA

    1. Refer to the screen tearing down steps (2.0） to disassembly the screen

    2. Unscrew two screws and remove the plastic cover

  1. Cut off the wire tie and unplug all the terminals

  1. Unscrews two screws on PCBA as marked ,then remove PCBA

  1. Tear down Battery（CL2019）

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~3.0 to disassembly the Screen & PCBA

    2. Unplug the terminal pins

  1. Unscrews 2pcs screws that be fixed the battery

  1. Remove the battery


  1. Tear down Battery（CL2371）

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~3.0to disassembly the Screen & PCBA

    2. Refer to the steps 6.0~8.0to disassembly the appliance housing

  1. Unscrew screws and disassembly battery cover

  1. Unscrew screws and unplug terminals to disassembly battery

  1. Tear down brush roller motor

    1. Unscrew screws and take out scraper strip

  1. Unscrew bottom screws and disassembly Assy power brush part top cover

  1. Unscrew screw and unplug connecter to remover brush roller motor

  1. Teard own Micro switch & Water pump

    1. Refer to the steps 10.1~10.2 to disassembly the assy power brush part

    2. Unscrew the screw and unplug switch terminal to replace it

  1. Unscrew the screw,unplug terminal and water pipe to replace pump

  1. Tear down Main Body（rotary union）

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~4.0 to disassembly the Screen & PCBA&battery

    2. Unscrew screws of the machine as bleow

  1. Unscrew 2pcs screws that located on the side of display screen

  1. Remove the decoration cover that located on the bottom of the machine, unscrew 1pcs screw

  1. Unscrew 4pcs screws of the bottom of the machine that connected to power brush

  1. Unscrew 1pcs screws of the machine and Remove rotary union

  1. Open the housing of machine

  1. Tear down Main motor

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~5.0 to disassembly the Screen & PCBA&battery&main body

    2. Take out main motor according to photos as below

  1. Tear down Water level sensor

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~5.0 to disassembly the Screen & PCBA&battery&main body

    2. Unplug water level sensor terminal and the water pipe to replace it

  1. Tear down sewage sensor（iloop）

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~5.0 to disassembly the Screen & PCBA&Battery&Main body

    2. Unscrew 2pcs screws and unplug the terminal to replace sewage sensor

  1. Tear down body charging wire

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~5.0 to disassembly the Screen & PCBA&Battery&Main body

    2. Pry up charging wire part to remove it

  1. Tear down Repair Charging

    1. Unscrew charging base screws

  1. Disassembly repair charging

Noted: If the machine to be repaired has charging contact issues, please replace both the repair charging and body charging wire( which tear down guide refer to the 9.1~9.2)
