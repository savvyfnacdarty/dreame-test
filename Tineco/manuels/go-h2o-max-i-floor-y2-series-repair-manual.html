Repair notice

Important Safety Instructions:
1. Wear gloves when carrying out any repair work.
2. Ensure that there are no exposed electrical wires in the maintenance environment and strictly prohibit direct testing of the equipment with AC power to prevent electric shock.
3. When repairing electrical components, please disconnect the battery pack first.

Preparation:
Empty the clean water tank and dirty water tank before disassembly.

Replacement requirements:
1. All parts that have passed the cross-check must be removed from the faulty machine, fitted with the user's original adapter and then subjected to the next replacement test.
2. After repair, organise the removed old parts and unused parts.

Wiring diagram

Tear down Guide

  1. Tear down handle& Accessory

    1. Pull the handle up after holding according to photos as below

  1. Disassembly all accessories

  1. Tear down Display Screen and Battery

    1. Use a tools like rubber mallet to light tap and disassemble Display Screen(9FWRD1D0000)

  1. Unplug terminals to disassemble battery（9FWPT1D0000）

  1. Tear down Assy Brush（Scraper/ Brush Motor/ Micro switch/ Pump/wire/ Repair Wheel）

    1. Remove 4 screws to disassemble the Scraper

  1. Unscrews screws to disassemble Assy brush cover

  1. Unplug terminal to disassemble the Brush Motor（9FWRM1D0000）

  1. Unplug terminal and remove screw to disassemble the Micro switch（9FWRK1D0000）

  1. Unplug pipe and remove screw to disassemble the Pump（9FWRU160000）

  1. Remove the tape and unplug terminal to disassemble the wire（9FWRI1D0000）

  1. Unplug pipe and remove screw to disassemble the Repair Wheel（9FWRW1D0000）

  1. Tear down Main Body（Rotary union/Plastic part/ Water valve/ Main Motor/Wire）

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~3.0

    2. Remove screws to disassemble the Assy brush part

  1. Remove lock latch to disassemble the Rotary union（9FWRH1D0100）

  1. Remove 2 screws to disassemble the plastic part（9FWRH1D0200）

  1. Remove Screws to disassemble the Assy front cover and Assy rear cover

  1. Remove Screws to disassemble the Water valve（9FWRH1D0300）

  1. Remove Screws to disassemble the Battery&Motor housing

  1. Remove Screws to disassemble the Main Motor（9FWRM1D0100）and Wire（9FWRI1D0100）
