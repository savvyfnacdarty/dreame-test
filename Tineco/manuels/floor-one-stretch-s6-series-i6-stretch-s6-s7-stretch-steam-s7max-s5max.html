Wiring diagram

Tear down Guide

  1. Teardown handle& Accessory

    1. Disassembly all accessories and Pull the handle up after holding

  1. Teardown Drying & Charging Dock(Dock PCBA)

    1. Remove the rubber and Unscrew 4pcs screws，disassembly bottom cover

  1. Unscrew 2pcs screws and disassembly Dock PCBA

  1. Teardown Drying & Charging Dock(Base Charging Wire)

    1. Refer to the steps 2.1 to disassembly the bottom cover

    2. Unscrew 6pcs screws and remove Assy Air Flow Part

  1. Unscrew screws and disassembly Base Charging Wire

  1. Teardown Assy Brush Part(Scraper& Brush PCBA)

    1. Unscrew 4pcs screws and disassembly scraper

  1. Unscrew Screws and disassembly cover

  1. Unscrew Screws and replug all terminal to replace Brush PCBA

  1. Teardown Assy Brush Part(Micro Switch& CWT Drain Valve)

    1. Refer to the steps 4.0 to disassembly the Scraper& Brush PCBA

    2. Unscrew Screws to replace Micro Switch

  1. Unplug pipe to replace CWT Drain Valve

  1. Teardown Assy Brush Part(Lay Flat Sensor & Water Level Sensor)

    1. Refer to the steps 4.0 to disassembly the Scraper& Brush PCBA

    2. Unscrew screws to replace Lay Flat Sensor

  1. Unplug pipe to replace Water Level Sensor

  1. Teardown Assy Brush Part(Brush Roller Motor & Pump& Spray Water Plate)

    1. Refer to the steps 4.0 to disassembly the Scraper& Brush PCBA

    2. Unscrew screws to replace Brush Roller Motor

  1. Unscrew screws and unplug pipe to replace Pump

  1. Unscrew screws and unplug pipe to replace Spray Water Plate

  1. Teardown Assy Brush Part(Body Charging Wire)

    1. Refer to the steps 4.0 to disassembly the Scraper& Brush PCBA

    2. Disassembly to replace Brush Roller Motor

  1. Teardown Rotary union

    1. Refer to the steps 4.0~8.0 to disassembly the Assy Brush Part

    2. Unscrew screws and disassembly Rotary union

  1. Teardown Assy Body Part(Display screen& Battery)

    1. Disassembly Body Rear Cover Top.

  1. Disassembly Body Top DECO ASSY.

  1. Clockwise rotation to teardown Display screen.

  1. Unscrew screws and disassembly display screen holder.

  1. Unplug terminal and disassembly to replace Battery.

  1. Teardown Assy Body Part(Main Motor&Main PCBA)

    1. Refer to the steps 4.0~8.0,9.0~10.5 to disassembly the Assy Brush Part & Display screen& Battery

    2. Disassembly Decorative Rubber and Unscrew screws.

  1. Unscrew screws and Disassembly Motor housing to replace Main Motor.

  1. Unscrew screws and Disassembly Body housing.

  1. Unscrew screws and unplug terminal to replace Main PCBA.

  1. Teardown Assy Body Part(DWT Signal Wire & Sewage Sensor)

    1. Refer to the steps 4.0~8.0,9.0~11.5, to disassembly the Assy Brush Part and Display screen& Battery and Main Motor&Main PCBA

    2. You can replace DWT Signal Wire now.

  1. Unscrew screws to replace Sewage Sensor

Note: When Main Motor Failure should be test whether lay flat sensor is failure
Test method

1.Turn on voice

2.Turn on appliance and lay flat it，Check whether there is a voice prompt on the first times lay flat?

Condition1：No voice →Lay Flat Sensor is failure→ please replace Lay Flat Sensor
Condition2：voice prompt：Rotate the body to the central position for lay-flat cleaning
→ Lay Flat Sensor is OK → don't need to replace
