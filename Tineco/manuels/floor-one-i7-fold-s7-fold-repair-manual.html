Tear down guide

  1. Tear down brush part

    1. Disassemble all accessories.

1.2 Remove the scraper

Remove the bottom screw of the floor brush. Remove the scraper

  1. Unscrew the other screws, open the upper cover of the brush

Remove the bottom screw of the floor brush.

Remove the floor brush cover decorative cover

Remove the screws in the hole position in the picture above.

Separate the upper cover of the floor brush.

2. Tear down rotary union

2.1Separate the floor brush base harness;

2.2Separate the fuselage from the brush assembly;

2.3Remove the screws in the picture

2.4 Separate adapter and fuselage

2.5 The position pointed by the arrow is glued, pry it off, and there is a screw inside.

2.6 Remove this screw

2.7 Remove the screw at the position indicated by the arrow

Separate body bottom cover and adapter assembly

. Disassembly of the main display screen

3.1Remove the handle from the 4 screws indicated in the picture above.

3.2Press the handle lock up and remove the screw plug as shown above.

3.3 Remove the screw，separate the rotating cover

3.4Use a one-word screwdriver to pry open the main display along the gap

3.5Separate the terminal, replace the main display screen

3.1 Remove the rubber plug at the handle connection，remove the screw

3.2 Remove the fuselage wheel and loosen the inner screws.

3.3 Use a screwdriver to pick the front cover of the light bar and loosen the screws inside.

  1. Separate the handle assembly from the fuselage

3.5 Use a screwdriver to open the display along the gap

4.Tear down main body

4.1 Disassembly of the upper cover of the fuselage

4.1.1 Remove the fuselage wheel with a screwdriver， remove the screw

4.1.2 Remove the two screws in the picture above, and remove the internal 4 screws.

4.1.3 Remove the back screw, Use force up the fuselage to separate the fuselage cover

4.1.4 Remove all the screws on the aluminum plate on the back side of the fuselage and remove the aluminum body (aluminum plate).

4.2 Replace the main control PCBA

To repair and replace the main control board, the floor brush base must be removed first。Due to the limited space position of the main control board, it is necessary to pull out a section of the combined wiring harness from the floor brush base to the side of the main control board, and then pull back the floor brush assembly after the maintenance and replacement.

4.2.1 Excess sealant on the cleaning fuselage

4.2.2 Remove the screw

4.2.3 Remove the upper cover of the PCB box, unplug the terminals, and remove the main control board.

4.3 Battery pack disassembly

4.3.1 Separate the wire harness plugged into the terminal of the battery pack and clean the RTV glue around the wire harness.

4.3.2 Remove the two screws that fix the battery pack and take out the battery pack.

4.4 Main motor disassembly

4.4.1 Remove the 4 screws inside.

4.4.2 Extract the fuselage bracket up and separate the fuselage bracket and fuselage.

4.4.3 Separate the upper cover of the motor cover and remove the main motor
