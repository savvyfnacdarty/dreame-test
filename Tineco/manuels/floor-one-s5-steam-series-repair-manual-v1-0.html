Repair notice

Important Safety Instructions：

1. Wear gloves when carrying out any repair work.

2. Ensure that there are no exposed electrical wires in the maintenance environment and strictly prohibit direct testing of the equipment with AC power to prevent electric shock.

3. When repairing electrical components, please disconnect the battery pack first.

Preparation:

Empty the clean water tank and dirty water tank before disassembly.

Replacement requirements:

1. All parts that have passed the cross-check must be removed from the faulty machine, fitted with the user's original adapter and then subjected to the next replacement test.

2. After repair, organise the removed old parts and unused parts.

Tear down Guide

  1. Tear down handle& Accessory

    1. Pull the handle up after holding according to photos as below

  1. Disassembly all accessories: clean water tank, dirty water tank, handle, brush roller cover, brush roller. ：

Tear down brush part

2.1 tear off the sticker, and unscrew screws

  1. Unscrew the screws and take out the scraper.

2.3 unscrew other screws and take out brush upper cover

  1. unscrew the 4pcs screws and take out the boiler

  1. unscrew the 4pcs screws and take out the boiler bottom cover

  1. Take out the water level sensor and reed switch

2.6 unscrew screws and take out brush roller motor

Tear down rotary union

3.1 refer to 1-2 to tear down brush part

3.2 unscrew screws and water pipe to release water pipe and wire

3.3 release bottom of Sewage water pipe

3.4 unscrew screws to disassemble black plastic part

3.5 unscrew left screws to take off rotary union

  1. Tear down Display Screen & PCBA

    1. Twist off the screen with hands.

  1. Pluck off terminal pins of display screen

  1. Unscrew the 3pcs screws and take out the cover

  1. Pluck off all terminal pins of wires

  1. Unscrew 2pcs outer screws

  1. Unscrew the 4pcs screws and take out the cover

  1. Remove the remaining screws, pluck off terminals and teardown the PCBA

5. Tear down main body & cable wire

5.1 refer to 1-4 to tear down display

5.2 Unscrew the 6pcs screws in the main body to release body cover

5.3 Use a slotted screwdriver to pry up the white plastic part

5.4 unscrew remaining screws, take out black plastic part, mute button & switch

5.5 Unscrew remaining screws and disassemble plastic part

5.6 Unscrew screws and take off cover

5.7 Take off wire and tear down Power Cord

6. tear down main motor

6.1 refer to 1-5 to tear down main body

6.2 Tear down wire cover from body part

6.3 remove remaining screws and take off motor

7. Tear down water pump and dirt sensor

7.1 refer to 1-6 to disassemble main body and motor

7.2 Unscrew screws and pluck off terminal pins

7.3 take off water pump

7.4 take off dirt sensor
