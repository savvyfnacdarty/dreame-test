Repair notice

Important Safety Instructions:
1. Wear gloves when carrying out any repair work.
2. Ensure that there are no exposed electrical wires in the maintenance environment and strictly prohibit direct testing of the equipment with AC power to prevent electric shock.
3. When repairing electrical components, please disconnect the battery pack first.

Preparation:
Empty the clean water tank and dirty water tank before disassembly.

Replacement requirements:
1. All parts that have passed the cross-check must be removed from the faulty machine, fitted with the user's original adapter and then subjected to the next replacement test.
2. After repair, organise the removed old parts and unused parts.

Tear down Guide

  1. Tear down handle& Accessory

    1. Pull the handle up after holding according to photos as below

  1. Disassembly all accessories

  1. Tear down Display Screen

    1. Pry up and disassemble Body Tube Deco Cover

  1. Remove screw and disassemble Body TOP DECO Cover

  1. Pry up and Clockwise rotation to teardown display screen

  1. Tear down Brush Top Cover

    1. Disassemble the Scraper and Small wheel

  1. Remove screws to disassemble the Brush Top Cover

  1. Tear down Brush roller Motor

    1. Refer to the steps 3.0 to disassemble Brush Top Cover

    2. Remove Screws to disassemble the Brush roller Motor

  1. Tear down Main Motor and PTC（Sewage /humidity sensor）

    1. Refer to the steps 3.0~4.0 to disassemble the Screen & PCBA

    2. Remove screws to disassemble the Suction Air Channel（Sewage sensor）

  1. Remove Screws and unplug terminal to disassemble the Main motor and PTC

  1. Unscrews and disassemble the humidity sensor


  1. Tear down Micro swtich(big wheel)

    1. Refer to the steps 3.0~5.0

    2. Unscrew screws to remove Micro swtich

  1. Unscrew screws and disassemble big wheel

  1. After disassemble big wheel,we can separate Assy brush part and Assy Body Part

  1. Tear down Assy Body Part(Plastic part/Wire/water level sensor/Pump/PCBA)

    1. Refer to the steps 3.0~6.0

    2. Unscrew screws to disassemble Body CWT Front Cover

  1. Unplug the pipe to disassemble water valve

  1. Pry up to disassemble the plastic part

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Body Front Cover and Body Rear Cover

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Wire

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Water level sensor

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Pump

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble PCBA
