Repair notice

Important Safety Instructions:
1. Wear gloves when carrying out any repair work.
2. Ensure that there are no exposed electrical wires in the maintenance environment and strictly prohibit direct testing of the equipment with AC power to prevent electric shock.
3. When repairing electrical components, please disconnect the battery pack first.

Preparation:
Empty the clean water tank and dirty water tank before disassembly.

Replacement requirements:
1. All parts that have passed the cross-check must be removed from the faulty machine, fitted with the user's original adapter and then subjected to the next replacement test.
2. After repair, organise the removed old parts and unused parts.

Tear down Guide

  1. Tear down handle& Accessory

    1. Pull the handle up after holding according to photos as below

  1. Disassembly all accessories

  1. Tear down Display Screen

    1. Remove screw and disassemble Body Rear Cover

  1. Remove screw and disassemble Body DECO Cover Top

  1. Clockwise rotation to teardown display screen

  1. Tear down Main PCBA

    1. Refer to the screen tearing down steps (2.0） to disassemble the screen

    2. Unscrew two screws and unplug the terminal to disassemble the Main PCBA

  1. Tear down Battery/Main Motor（CL2123）

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~3.0 to disassemble the Screen & PCBA

    2. Remove 4 screws to disassemble the Body Front cover

  1. Unscrews 2pcs screws to disassemble this part

  1. Unplug terminal and disassemble the hook to disassemble the Main motor and Battery

  1. Tear down Battery/Main Motor（CL2335）

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~3.0 to disassemble the Screen & PCBA

    2. Remove 4 screws to disassemble the Body Front cover

  1. Remove 4 screws to disassemble the Main motor and Battery


  1. Tear down Assy brush part(Scraper/LED LAMP PCBA/Brush PCBA/ Brush Roller Motor/ Micro swtich/ Pump/assit repair wheel)

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~4.0to disassemble the Screen & PCBA

    2. Unscrew screws to remove Scraper

  1. Unscrew screws and disassemble Assy Brush Cover

  1. Unscrew screws and disassemble LED LAMP PCBA

  1. Unscrew screws and unplug terminals to disassemble Brush PCBA

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Brush Roller Motor

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Micro swtich

  1. Unscrew screws and unplug water pipe to disassemble the Pump

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble the Swivel connctor cover R/L

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble the Assist Repair Wheel

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble the Assy brush part

  1. Tear down Assy Main Body

    1. Refer to the steps 2.0~5.0to disassemble the Screen\PCBA\Battery\Main Motor/Assybrush part

    2. Unscrew bottom screws and disassembly Body DECO cover bottom

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Rotary union

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Body Front and Body Rear

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Sewage sensor

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Wire

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Water level sensor

  1. Unscrew screws to disassemble Electrolytic water wire
