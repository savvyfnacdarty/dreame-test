Tear down Guide

  1. Teardown handle& Accessory

    1. Disassembly all accessories and Pull the handle up after holding


  1. Teardown Assy Brush Part（Scraper water board& scraper）

    1. Unscrew 4pcs screws and disassembly Scraper water board

  1. Unscrew 4pcs screws and disassembly scraper

  1. Unscrew Screws and disassembly cover

  1. Teardown Assy Brush Part（Brush roller motor,Micro switch&pump）

    1. Refer to the steps 2.2~2.3 to disassembly the Scraper water board& scraper

    2. Disassembly brush roller motor deco.

  1. Unscrew and unplug terminal to disassembly brush roller motor

  1. Unscrew and unplug terminal to disassembly Micro Switch

  1. Unscrew and unplug terminal&pipe to disassembly pump

  1. Unscrew and unplug terminal to disassembly Electrolytic water module

  1. Teardown Rotary union

    1. Refer to the steps 2.2~2.3,3.1~3.6 to disassembly the parts

    2. Unscrew screws and disassembly Rotary union

  1. Teardown Display Screen

    1. Disassembly Decorative Rubber and Unscrew screws

    2. Unplug terminal,disassembly hooks to replace Display screen

  1. Teardown main body housing

    1. Refer to the steps 2.2~2.3,3.1~3.6,4.0,5.0 to disassembly the parts

    2. Unscrew screws to disassembly body housing

  1. Teardown PCBA,Water level sensor,Main Motor,Battery,Wire

    1. Refer to the steps 2.2~2.3,3.1~3.6,4.0,5.0,6.0 to disassembly the parts

    2. Unscrew and disassembly PCBA cover

  1. Unplug terminal (Noted:don’t misplug when you assembly PCBA) to disassembly PCBA

  1. Unplug pipe to replace Water level sensor

  1. Unscrew and disassembly Main Motor

  1. Unscrew and disassembly Battery

  1. Unscrew and disassembly Wire
